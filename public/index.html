Your handwriting font

Currently using —

Pick one of the bundled handwriting fonts. Applies instantly to every note — and to PDF/PNG exports.

Upload one image of your handwriting (a paragraph or a notes page, ideally each character visible at least once). The open-source HandFonted pipeline runs locally on your machine: it segments characters with PaddleOCR, classifies each one with a trained model, and assembles a real TTF.

Heads up: first build takes ~30–60 seconds. Characters missing from your sample fall back to a default handwriting font. Requires a one-time Python setup — see the README.
